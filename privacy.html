Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Personal Secretary is a private tool used by one person, its owner, on his own Google accounts. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): reads the owner's incoming messages to find things he needs to do, and may save a reply as a draft in his own mailbox. It does not send email.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): reads the owner's events and creates events on his own calendar.

How the data is used

The data is used only to maintain the owner's own to-do list and calendar. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Where it goes

  • The software runs on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored in that computer's macOS Keychain.
  • Message text is sent to Anthropic's Claude service for analysis, to decide whether it contains a task.
  • The resulting tasks (titles and short notes) are written to the owner's own TickTick account.

Nothing is shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Personal Secretary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
